== sequential.stat
conditional [error]: typeI(trial)
  Weakest part: cited from the literature; not machine-checked (interim, final)
  Evidence: combination proved in Lean; the members' own guarantees are assumed; guarantee: finite-sample bound
  The look levels sum to 1/40, which does not exceed the overall error bound 1/40.
  If the test at each look rejects the true null with probability at most its level, the probability of rejecting it at any look is at most 1/40, whatever the dependence between looks. This split is conservative: it does not use the correlation between looks.
  Not checked here: each look's own error bound, listed as an assumption.
  Assumptions:
    The looks happen at the declared cumulative sample sizes, fixed in advance, and the study stops at the first look that rejects.
    interim: Null rejection probability is at most 3/2000 whenever mean_treatment - mean_control <= 0. (cited from the literature; not machine-checked)
    final: Null rejection probability is at most 47/2000 whenever mean_treatment - mean_control <= 0. (cited from the literature; not machine-checked)
