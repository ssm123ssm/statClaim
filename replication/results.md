## Notes

- `undeclared_peeking`: StatClaim sees only the declaration. The report lists 'run once' as declared by the file, which is the statement the study broke.
- `same_outcome_declared_twice`: The outcome is named. Without a name, two declarations with identical content are indistinguishable from two real outcomes measured on the same participants.
- `approximate_sample_size`: The size is computed in scenarios.py from the standard formula, not typed in.
- `non_normal_outcomes`: Nothing in a design can show the outcome's distribution. The report lists normality as assumed.
- `small_sample_large_sample_test`: No sample size is too small to declare. The result is worded as a large-sample approximation with no bound at the declared size.
- `risk_margin_logistic`: The logistic test is of a coefficient. A margin on the risk difference is a different question, and a rule that combines an odds-ratio threshold with a risk-difference margin cannot be stated.
- `lower_is_better_undeclared`: The register lists that higher values are taken to be better, marked assumed. Declaring better="lower" makes this a rejection.
- `repeated_measures_as_participants`: Nothing in the file says that observations share participants. The register lists independent observations as assumed. Declaring the outcome's measurements makes this a rejection.
- `cluster_summaries_on_participants`: Before the cluster-level test had a contract, this analysis could only be a declared test, and the mistake was invisible.
- `cluster_randomised_undeclared`: Nothing in the file says that clinics were randomised. The register lists independent observations as assumed. Declaring cluster_assignment(...) makes this a rejection.
- `endpoint_chosen_after_interim`: A verified test of the chosen endpoint does not cover the choice. The report's justification lists that the analysis was chosen before outcomes were observed: the statement the study broke.
- `per_protocol_population`: The file states who is analysed. Without population=..., the exclusions stay invisible: nothing in the file describes them, and the guarantee holds for the declared numbers.
