# Community rules

Be kind, specific and open to correction. Discuss ideas and evidence without
personal attacks. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Harassment, discrimination, threats and sharing private information are not allowed.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation.
Report concerns privately to ssm123ssm@gmail.com; avoid posting private details
in public issues. Maintainers will review reports fairly and keep details
private where possible.
